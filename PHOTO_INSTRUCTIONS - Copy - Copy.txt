HOW TO ADD & REPLACE PHOTOS FOR KIRSTY'S WEBSITE
===================================================

Currently, Kirsty's photo (the one on campus steps in the green #12 jersey) is already loaded as:
- hero.jpg (The main hero portrait)
- photo01.jpg through photo08.jpg (The Scrapbook Photo Wall polaroids)

HOW TO ADD MORE OF YOUR REAL PHOTOS:
1. Simply copy your photos into this folder ("images/kirsty/").
2. Rename them to:
   - photo01.jpg (First polaroid)
   - photo02.jpg (Second polaroid)
   - photo03.jpg (Third polaroid)
   - photo04.jpg (Fourth polaroid)
   - photo05.jpg (Fifth polaroid)
   - photo06.jpg (Sixth polaroid)
   - photo07.jpg (Seventh polaroid)
   - photo08.jpg (Eighth polaroid)

HOW TO EDIT THE CAPTIONS & DATES:
1. Open "js/data.js" in any text editor (Notepad, VS Code, etc.).
2. Find the "photos" section.
3. Change the "caption", "date", or "note" for any photo!
